Recap: Escalation of Force

Activity Update

Increase in activity has been marced; threats have been backed by an increase in activity and an escalation of force. There have been increased kinetic engagements around the ports and on Montana Jacques.

Smuggling Operation

FMC resources are expanding and proving rapidly more sophisticated, with Narco Sub operations being seen across the Los Marmalingas Archipelago

Cartel Retaliation

Escalation of Capabilities

Cartel show a clear escalation of force with advanced capabilities. An FMC helicopter was used to carry out an attack against a Policia Nacional operation in one of their farms, taking 8 lives.

Civil Unrest

The FMC have been targeting local businesses refusing to pay into their protection racketeering operation, causing extreme public disorder throughout Dos Cervezas

Increasing Recruitment

Mass Recruitment

Aerial footage shows a huge recruitment drive of people up through Montana Jacques

Training Camps

Permanent training facilities appear to have been erected in the jungle

Infrastructure

Runways and permanent forward operating bases show clear signs that the FMC are mounting some form of takeover, using the mountains and jungles as a base of operations

Projections

Strategic Forecast

  • Increased activity, both in civil areas and in narcotics movements throughout the country
  • Direct aggressions towards civilians and police forces appear to be escalating
  • The FMC has started to behave like a paramilitary force, using narcotics money as funding. It is possible that El Jefe and his operatives are going to make an attempt to seize power within Dos Cervezas, and potentially further afield
